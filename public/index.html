LEGAL + COMPLIANCE PROTOTYPE

Market Surveillance Brief

A one-click, factual market-data brief for compliance teams monitoring listed entities.

Human review required
Educational prototype, not legal or investment advice. A market movement is not proof of material information, insider trading, or a disclosure breach. Use this as a prompt to investigate facts through the proper compliance process.

1. Select a monitored company

No event narrative or legal questionnaire is needed. The tool retrieves NSE data and prepares a plain-English review brief.

This demo has 10 companies. A real internal tool could run automatically every morning for the company watchlist.

What the tool checks

  • 30-day trading-volume profile and recent-volume change
  • 52-week price-range position
  • Corporate actions that may distort raw price comparisons

2. Receive a review alert

Choose a company and run the brief. The output is factual and timestamped, ready to support—not replace—compliance review.

Data source: NSE Bhavcopy MCP. Intended only as a free, informational/educational demonstration under NSE’s stated MCP limitations.
